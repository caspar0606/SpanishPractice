Help

Quick guide. Use the walkthrough for step-by-step instructions.

1) What to do next

  • Three cards suggest a next exercise: something you need, the next step on the roadmap, and something aimed at your goal.
  • Tap a card to start. Use Something else if you want to pick the type and focus yourself.
  • A Vocab review card opens a short word quiz instead of a full exercise.

2) Something else

  • Writing: write a short response and get corrections.
  • Reading: answer comprehension questions and get feedback.
  • Listening: a short dialogue to play, then comprehension questions. The transcript appears after you submit.
  • Speaking: record a short answer. We mark grammar, not pronunciation.
  • Drills: short questions targeting a specific focus area.
  • Weak areas: uses your progress to pick what to practise.
  • Preferences: you choose the exact focus.

Learn, Ask, Vocab

  • Learn: tense tables and grammar notes you can browse any time.
  • Ask: a grounded tutor that answers from those notes — not a conversation partner.
  • Vocab: words harvested from your exercises. Star, ignore, or review them.

Drills

  • Only used if you open Something else and choose Drills — tapping a card skips this.
  • First pick Tenses or Grammar. Drills cannot mix both.
  • Use my progress then picks your weak points. I'll choose lets you tick the specific ones.

During practice

  • Use the Focus tab to see what this exercise targets.
  • Tap a Spanish word in a prompt, passage, or transcript for an English gloss.
  • After a few exercises, My progress appears so you can see how you are doing.
